## Limits

The Mac check proves its existing platform/model path, not a second SDLC install.
Do not point separate Mac and Proxmox pollers at the same issue queue: their
Kubernetes Leases do not coordinate across clusters. Workplace installation
still requires its own credentials, model route, CRD compatibility, network
policy, CI executor and end-to-end canary.

Tester evidence remains a PM-recorded reply, not an independently verified test
artifact. Durable A2A task recovery, per-issue queue isolation and CI wait limits
remain open hardening items. The separate CI agent-quality-gate bundle still
needs its bridge and isolated generated-test executor implemented; ordinary CI
success here does not prove that design.
