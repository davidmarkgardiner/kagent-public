# Email draft: token allocation increase

**Subject:** Request to increase our monthly Copilot token allocation (10,000 → 20,000)

Hi [Manager],

I'd like to request an increase to our monthly GitHub Copilot token allocation, from 10,000 to 20,000. The full business case is attached.

**The problem**
We keep running out of tokens before the month ends. Our team's day-to-day development relies on agents. When the allocation runs out, we can't build, iterate or deliver until it resets.

**Why we use so many**
- We do a lot of feature development and proof-of-concept work in the AI space.
- New AI applications keep coming along, and we have to work out how each one works and integrates with our current systems. That means a lot of trial and error.

**What we're already doing to keep usage down**
- Luna, the cheapest model, is our default and covers about 90% of our usage.
- We use Sol or Terra only for complex or difficult tasks.
- We use it only for development and building, not for debugging or general engineering work.
- We're staying within the rules and the allocation still doesn't last the month.

**Usage history**
Usage has been this high even in months with reduced capacity. In one month I lost about two weeks to a dev environment outage and couldn't use or borrow my tokens. The usage history is available if you want to check any of this.

**The ask**
Approve an increase to 20,000 tokens per month, effective [date]. We'll keep Luna as the default, report the model split each month, and review the increase after [3 months].

Happy to walk through the usage data or answer questions.
