## Reuse on SharePoint or Viva Engage

1. Use the title and body in `article.md` or copy the rendered `article.html`. Keep the eight numbered chapters and source references.
2. Upload `images/01-cover.png` as the cover. Place `02-two-boundaries.png` after chapter 2 and `03-every-connection.png` below the chapter 6 heading. Apply the captions and alt text from `image-notes.md`.
3. Review the two tables in the destination editor. If tables do not paste cleanly, use a list with one request and its identity/permission or expected outcome per item; preserve the distinction between intended tests and observed results.
4. Preview the internal page and its image placement, then publish through your normal internal process.
5. Use `viva-engage-introduction.txt` to introduce the internal article. Replace the URL placeholder before posting. If your tenant exposes SharePoint News-to-Engage cross-posting and you have the required permissions, that is another way to share the same article.

A practical option is to keep the full article as SharePoint News and use Viva Engage for the introduction and discussion. Microsoft documents rich-format SharePoint News and the cross-posting flow here:

- https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/pages-in-sharepoint/use-the-news-web-part-on-a-sharepoint-page
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/engage/cross-post-sharepoint-news-viva-engage
