# Proposed objectives for the 2026 self-review

Prepared on 4 November 2026 from repository material, relevant saved working history and the review context supplied by the author.

These objectives are being documented retrospectively. They describe the intended focus of the work and proposed targets; they are not a record of objectives formally agreed at the start of the year. The review period is assumed to be the 2026 calendar year. The medium- and long-term objectives carry the capability developed this year into subsequent years.

Category definitions: **Short term:** less than 1 year; **Medium term:** 1 to 3 years; **Long term:** over 3 years. The proposed completion dates fall within these horizons from the preparation date.

## Three-year career direction and research basis

By autumn 2029, the aim is to demonstrate readiness to lead an internal AI Forward Deployed Engineering team: a hands-on technical leader who works alongside business specialists, delivers useful systems, develops engineers and turns successful implementations into reusable capabilities. The 2030 objective extends that direction into an established capability and continuing responsibility for emerging technology strategy.

The proposed direction for the bank is to embed AI in existing software delivery, SRE operations, security and compliance, governed data access and suitable business-service workflows. Shared platform services would support those teams with identity, tools, knowledge, evaluation and reliable execution. These are proposed priorities for the review, to be refined with business owners.

Research consulted on 4 November 2026:

- **Microsoft's FDE perspective**, written by its CVP of Forward Deployed Engineering, describes small multidisciplinary teams combining hands-on delivery, operational ownership, governance, capability transfer and measurable workflow outcomes. This supports positioning the objective around embedding with domain teams and building lasting internal capability. https://commandline.microsoft.com/thinking-about-the-evolution-of-forward-deployed-engineers-over-the-last-24-months/
- **OpenAI's FDE overview** describes working alongside users to understand workflows, integrate systems, evaluate solutions, move into production and share reusable capabilities. This supports the progression from experimentation towards adopted, supported workflows. https://openai.com/business/forward-deployed-engineering/
- **OpenAI's Deployment Lead role** emphasises delivery planning, cross-team coordination, adoption, reusable patterns and evaluations, and measurement against business goals. It provides a useful leadership competency reference alongside the objective's mentoring and people-development goals. https://openai.com/careers/deployment-lead-fde-nyc-new-york-city/

## Suggested opening for the eventual self-review

My focus during 2026 has been to develop practical expertise in AI and translate that learning into patterns the bank can assess and adopt. I have worked across agent frameworks and harnesses, secure model and tool access, Kubernetes runtime isolation, observability, evaluation and token efficiency. I have developed prototypes, technical assessments and reusable handovers for SRE triage, software delivery and governed data access, and explored compliance and vulnerability use cases. This has helped build an evidence base for architecture and investment decisions and identify the controls, operational ownership and validation needed for wider adoption.

When completing the review, add the applicable workplace delivery records, stakeholder feedback and measured outcomes to the relevant objective. Do not mark an objective achieved solely because the associated repository folder exists. Any proposed counts or dates above are new targets, not historical commitments or achieved metrics.
