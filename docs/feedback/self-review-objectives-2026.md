# Proposed objectives for the 2026 self-review

Prepared on 4 November 2026 from repository material, relevant saved working history and the review context supplied by the author.

These objectives are being documented retrospectively. They describe the intended focus of the work and proposed targets; they are not a record of objectives formally agreed at the start of the year. The review period is assumed to be the 2026 calendar year. The medium- and long-term objectives carry the capability developed this year into subsequent years.

Category definitions: **Short term:** less than 1 year; **Medium term:** 1 to 3 years; **Long term:** over 3 years. The proposed completion dates fall within these horizons from the preparation date.

## Objectives

| Objective name | Objective description | Target completion date | Category |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Develop applied AI expertise and an adoption roadmap | Build practical expertise in AI agents, models, tools and orchestration, and assess their suitability for use within the bank. Compare HolmesGPT, kagent, LangGraph, Codex and relevant agent harnesses; document architectural trade-offs and prioritise business use cases. Share findings through technical briefings, demonstrations and collaboration with engineering teams, stakeholders and Microsoft. Deliver an options assessment and a prioritised adoption roadmap. | 31 December 2026 | Short term |
| Define and validate secure, observable and cost-controlled agent foundations | Establish a reference architecture and control baseline for agents, model access and tools. Cover Agent Gateway, identity and authorisation, least privilege, network and runtime isolation, human approval, auditability, observability and evaluation. Validate representative permitted and denied access paths, and define controls and measurements for tool output size, context growth and token consumption. Record test evidence and outstanding risks. | 31 December 2026 | Short term |
| Demonstrate practical AI use cases for engineering and operations | Develop and evaluate bounded proofs of concept for SRE triage, the software delivery lifecycle and governed database/compliance access, alongside a design for CVE exposure assessment. Produce repeatable demonstrations, evaluation cases, implementation handovers and clear records of limitations. Define measures for answer quality, delivery or investigation time, reliability and token consumption to support decisions about further adoption. | 31 December 2026 | Short term |
| Establish a resilient, reusable Kubernetes agent platform | Partner with platform and security teams to take the reference architecture through an approved operational pilot. Evaluate suitable runtime patterns, including Agent Substrate, for isolation, session persistence, suspend/resume, recovery and efficient compute use. Integrate curated knowledge retrieval and governed memory as separate platform capabilities. Deliver a versioned onboarding pattern, support model, runbooks and evidence against agreed reliability, security and cost criteria. | 31 December 2027 | Medium term |
| Embed AI into priority engineering and control workflows | Work with SRE, engineering, security, compliance and data owners to take at least two prioritised use cases from proof of concept through the approved adoption process into supported use. Agree ownership, controls and baseline measures before rollout, and assess outcomes against those baselines. Use operational feedback to improve the workflows and report demonstrated benefits, costs, risks and adoption. | 31 December 2028 | Medium term |
| Lead sustainable and responsible AI engineering adoption | Develop into a trusted technical lead for the bank's use of AI in engineering and operations. Help shape the technology roadmap and reusable standards, mentor colleagues, share practical knowledge and maintain effective relationships with Microsoft and other relevant partners. Support investment and architecture decisions with evidence, and establish a recurring review of business value, reliability, security, responsible use and cost. | 31 December 2030 | Long term |

## Suggested opening for the eventual self-review

My focus during 2026 has been to develop practical expertise in AI and translate that learning into patterns the bank can assess and adopt. I have worked across agent frameworks and harnesses, secure model and tool access, Kubernetes runtime isolation, observability, evaluation and token efficiency. I have developed prototypes, technical assessments and reusable handovers for SRE triage, software delivery and governed data access, and explored compliance and vulnerability use cases. This has helped build an evidence base for architecture and investment decisions and identify the controls, operational ownership and validation needed for wider adoption.

When completing the review, add the applicable workplace delivery records, stakeholder feedback and measured outcomes to the relevant objective. Do not mark an objective achieved solely because the associated repository folder exists. Any proposed counts or dates above are new targets, not historical commitments or achieved metrics.
